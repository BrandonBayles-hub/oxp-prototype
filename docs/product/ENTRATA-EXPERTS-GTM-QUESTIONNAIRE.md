## 1. Product Definition & Positioning

**1.1 In one sentence, what is Entrata Experts?**
*Why it matters: the elevator pitch every seller repeats.*
> `[DRAFT]` The conversational front door to Autonomous Property Management — a governed, chat-first AI hub in OXP where operators ask plain-language questions about their portfolio and get cited, real-time answers.

**1.2 What category does it compete in, and how do we name that category?**
*Why it matters: category framing shapes the buyer's mental model and the competitive set.*
> `[OPEN]` (e.g., "governed conversational analytics," "AI copilot for property operations")

**1.3 What is the core problem we solve, in the customer's words?**
*Why it matters: messaging must echo the pain, not the feature.*
> `[DRAFT]` Operators sit on a rich PMS but can't get a straight answer out of it — insight is trapped in static dashboards, recurring questions get re-answered by hand, and the AI tools creeping in are ungoverned.

**1.4 What is our one-line differentiator vs. "just another chatbot"?**
*Why it matters: this is the wedge against fast-follower competitors.*
> `[DRAFT]` The first *governed* conversational layer — cited, portfolio-scoped answers with spend caps, model policy, and a full audit trail, plus a path from "ask" to "automation."

**1.5 What are we explicitly NOT (scope boundaries / anti-positioning)?**
*Why it matters: prevents over-promising and mis-qualified deals.*
> `[OPEN]` (e.g., not a replacement for the Analytics Platform; not a resident-facing chatbot; not a system of record)
